It covers planning through handover while excluding geographic doorway variants, thin brand reviews, and pages that duplicate `/services/`, `/merek/`, the 13 existing brand routes, or location sales pages. Technical, safety, legal, performance, compatibility, certification, warranty, price, and product statements require current primary evidence and, where appropriate, review by a qualified specialist.
